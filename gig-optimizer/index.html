    About this prototype. ShiftSmart's recommendations come from a transparent earnings model: published platform pay structures, hour-of-week demand curves from rideshare/delivery research, plus weather and event modifiers. Major gig platforms (Uber, DoorDash, Lyft, Instacart, etc.) do not expose public APIs for live earnings or zone demand, so this prototype simulates those inputs rather than scraping carrier accounts. A production deployment would layer in driver-reported earnings, opt-in trip telemetry, and licensed traffic/event feeds.
