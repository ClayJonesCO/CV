    About this prototype. Peakr's recommendations come from a transparent earnings model: published platform pay structures, hour-of-week demand curves from rideshare/delivery research, plus weather and event modifiers. Major gig platforms (Uber, DoorDash, Lyft, Instacart, etc.) do not expose public APIs for live earnings or zone demand, so this prototype simulates those inputs rather than scraping carrier accounts. The community data layer, sign-up bonuses, and the forecast's live feed are seeded/simulated here; production would pool anonymized driver-reported earnings on a backend (the data flywheel), use real affiliate/referral programs, and add opt-in trip telemetry and licensed traffic/event feeds. Logging is advisory and driver-sourced — Peakr never automates or scrapes platform accounts.
